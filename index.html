⌘T · Translate

Two answers, side by side.

Apple's Translation framework answers on your Mac. With the local model on, Gemma 4 E2B translates the same English beside it, and usually first: 0.40 s against Apple's 1.8 s for ten words.

Each answer is a card, and the words the two disagree on are underlined, so you see where the meaning is in doubt. Settings → Translate with picks Apple + Gemma, Apple only, or Gemma only. Thai → English is always Apple.

Sentences and answers on this page come from the app's 76-row Thai test corpus, as each engine answered them. Neither is always right: on its own, Gemma sometimes misspells Thai that Apple never does. That is why you see both.

⌘G · Check

Proofread your own English, gently.

Select a sentence you wrote, anywhere, and press ⌘G. Languagly lists each mistake, then the corrected sentence, then the same sentence in five tenses as a reference.

When your English is already right, it says so and changes nothing. Getting that right took measuring: an early prompt invented a mistake in every correct sentence it saw.

    Why the most precise model lost. Qwen altered only one correct sentence, but missed 34 of the 126 mistakes, against Gemma's 10. For a learner a miss is the worse failure, because silence reads as approval.

    The retired models were timed in August, before the instruction cache, so their times are not comparable.

    What the numbers do not say yet. Concept coverage says whether the meaning arrived, not whether the Thai reads well. The labels were drafted, not written by a Thai reader, and the blind side-by-side sheet for a Thai reader has not been judged yet.
    What it gave up. To stop inventing mistakes in correct English, the checker no longer reports pure register problems, like “please kindly advise”. A rule that has no measured benefit is taken out.

    Private by default

    Your sentences stay on your Mac.

    • On the device, offline. Apple's Translation framework and the local model both run on your Mac. Translation works with no network at all.
    • The local model is opt-in. Turn it on in Settings when you want it. Without it, translation is free and still offline.
    • Cloud models only if you ask. API mode is off by default. If you turn it on, your keys live in the Keychain, never in a settings file or a backup.
    • No account. Nothing to sign up for, and nothing synced anywhere.

    Requirements

    What you need.

    Required

    A Mac with Apple Silicon

    Built for arm64 only. The local model needs unified memory, so an Intel Mac is refused at install rather than given a feature that silently does nothing.

    Required

    macOS 15 Sequoia or newer

    Apple's on-device translation needs it. The first translation triggers Apple's one-time language download.

    Optional · the local model

    3.6 GB, once

    Downloaded once and repacked to 2.05 GB, with about 5.6 GB free while that runs. It needs about 2.5 GB of free memory while loaded. Translation itself needs none of this.
